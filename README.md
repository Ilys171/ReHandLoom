# ReHand Loom

A wearable hand-and-wrist rehabilitation system for rural Azerbaijani carpet weavers. A sensor glove tracks finger flexion, grip pressure and wrist motion during weaving or guided exercise, and streams session summaries to an app for home rehabilitation and remote specialist review.

Presented at the Azerbaijan Carpet Museum, 2026, and acknowledged by the Ministry of Culture of the Republic of Azerbaijan.

**Team:** Youssef El Haroun (Hardware & Embedded Systems) · Mikayil Ahmadbayli (Biomechanics & Rehabilitation) · Ilyas Taghiyev (Digital Systems & Data)
